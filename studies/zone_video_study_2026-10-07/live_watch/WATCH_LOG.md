### Pattern summary so far (owner's thesis, 3m view)
BUY at a 4H floor: price dives into the 4H box/level → sweeps the nearby lows → (often a failed first push) → floor holds with higher lows (often at a new 4H candle open) → a strong green 3m candle closes back above the small range / leaves an FVG = bias flips up → target the next 4H box.
SELL at a 4H ceiling (mirror): rally into the next 4H box → sweep of the equal highs / double top (often at a session open) → strong red 3m candle closes back below → lower high → break of the small range low → target the next 4H box down.
