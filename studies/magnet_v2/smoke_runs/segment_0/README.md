# Magnet research run

UNVERIFIED EXPLORATORY RUN. These numbers do not validate trading performance.

No 100% profitability or daily objective is established. One contract; gross and net values are USD. Signals include risk/portfolio rejects and descriptive forward excursions, not independent trades. Trades crossing missing bars are excluded from aggregate metrics. Open data-end positions are censored. Intrabar exit times are bar-close labels, not known execution timestamps. Entries are conditional decisions at the next opening print: risk and stop-gap checks may cancel an entry. This models an opening-price-aware decision, not a blind market order submitted at the prior close. MFE/MAE excludes all exit-bar extrema; these are conservative observed full-bar excursions, not exact intrabar excursions. A $30/oz excursion is 30 price points, approximately $300 gross on one MGC contract, not $30 P&L.
